I built this game with Claude 🎮

It's a conveyor-belt puzzle game: tap a belt to turn it, press Run, and get every package to the dock of its colour. The packages are little characters who react to what you build, and there are 36 puzzles across 5 worlds, with hints that teach you one step at a time.

How it was made: I wrote the game idea and the briefs, Claude Code wrote the code, and I kept reviewing it and sending it back with problems to fix and things to improve. Everything in this video is real gameplay from the actual builds, including the first playable version next to the latest one.

It's still in development and not on the app stores yet.
