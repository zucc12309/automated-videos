# Stop your coding agent from breaking your UI

Replace 'make the dashboard look better' with a scoped, checkable prompt.

## Step by step

1. **The vague prompt** — Most people type something like: make the dashboard look better. The agent has to guess what you mean.
2. **The better prompt** — Instead, give it a scope, constraints, and a way to check the result.
3. **Why it works** — Why it works: scope: one file, constraints: props and tokens, and check: review the diff.
4. **Expected kind of change** — Here's the kind of focused change you should expect back.
