# The future of AI-powered coding

As agents take over typing, the valuable work moves to intent, review and judgment.

## Step by step

1. **From request to tested change** — You describe the change. An agent plans it, edits the files, and runs the tests.
2. **Review the diff** — Your job shifts to review: reading diffs instead of typing boilerplate.
3. **Intent, architecture, judgment** — Teams will spend more time on intent, architecture, and judgment.
4. **What gets valuable** — Typing speed matters less. Clear thinking matters more.
5. **Only as good as your tests** — The catch: an agent is only as good as your tests and your review.
